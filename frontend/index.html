How it works

  1. 1

    Predict

    A computer copy of the real roads (OpenStreetMap, simulated in SUMO) is tuned until its trip times match TomTom's measurements. Then it shows where queues build up and why.

    SimulateIdentify
  2. 2

    Mitigate first

    Try the cheap fixes before the expensive ones: signal timing, a one-way side road, a wider approach. Only then a flyover, and always check what it does to the next junction.

    SimulatePredict
  3. 3

    Build what was tested, on the record

    A reviewer re-tests the options with more or less traffic, a decider approves with a written reason, and each step is fingerprinted so the record cannot be changed quietly.

    CompareDecide
