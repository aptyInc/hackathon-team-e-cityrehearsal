About the data

Every number on these screens is either measured in the real world or produced by our simulation. This page says which is which, where each one comes from, and how far to trust it.

How to read the tags

On every number

REAL
Measured in the real world: by TomTom (from probe vehicles) or by a published traffic study.
SIMULATED
Produced by our simulation of the roads. Only as good as its calibration.
SAMPLE DATA
A saved example result (the API in mock mode), not a simulation of what you asked.

On every input to the simulation

counted / measured
Someone measured it: a traffic count, a study, a probe speed or travel time.
estimated
Worked out from measured data, e.g. TomTom's vehicles-per-hour figure, which is its model's estimate.
calibrated
Tuned by us until the simulation's trip times match TomTom's. Fitted, not measured.
assumed
A reasonable guess with no data behind it yet, e.g. the split between cars and autos.

OpenStreetMap roads MAP

The road network the simulation drives on: lanes, speed limits, one-way streets and junction shapes, from the free, volunteer-made OpenStreetMap. It is a reference map, not a measurement: where it is wrong (a missing lane, an odd junction shape), the simulation is too.

referenceused on: every simulation, the route line on the corridor map

Overture building footprints MAP

About 16,400 building outlines along the corridor (Overture Maps, release 2026-09-23.1) and 3,370 around YMCA Circle. They are only used to draw the 3D city. Most buildings have no recorded height, so heights are assumed from the footprint size (floors × 3.2 m). The simulation does not use them.

footprints: mappedheights: mostly assumed

Open-Meteo rain REAL

≈ +5% trip time while it is raining (uncertain)

Hourly rainfall for July 2026 at three points on the route (a weather reanalysis, not a rain gauge), compared with TomTom's daily trip times for 1–15 July. Rainy weekdays were not measurably slower as a whole (61.0 vs 62.0 min). Our best estimate is about 5% slower while it is actually raining, but the data allow anything from 11% faster to 21% slower, so treat it as a rough guide. One stretch, Gachibowli Circle → Biodiversity junction, was slower on every rainy day compared.

rain: measured (modelled)effect on traffic: estimated

YMCA Circle traffic count, 2020 study REAL

Vehicles per hour by type on each road into YMCA Circle, from a published study (IJRAR, June 2020). Used for the YMCA close-up's traffic mix. Traffic has grown since: the 2.0× growth to 2026 is estimated by matching TomTom's speeds.

counts: countedgrowth to 2026: estimated

Words used on this page

TomTom
A company that makes maps and collects traffic data from millions of phones, cars and navigation devices. We use two of its products: Traffic Stats (how long trips took) and Junction Analytics (what is happening at a junction now).
Probe vehicle
Any vehicle that sends its anonymous GPS position to a company like TomTom. TomTom sees only a sample of all traffic: speeds and travel times from probes are reliable; vehicle counts are TomTom's estimate.
Stretch (leg)
The road between two neighbouring junctions, e.g. Tolichowki → Nanal Nagar. The whole trip is the sum of 12 stretches.
Approach
One road coming into a junction. A crossroads has four. TomTom reports delay and queue per approach.
Simulation
A computer copy of the roads (SUMO, an open-source traffic simulator) where thousands of virtual two-wheelers, cars, autos and buses drive, stop at signals and queue. We change the copy, not the real roads.
Calibration
Tuning the simulation until it behaves like the real roads: here, adjusting speed limits per stretch, signal green times and traffic volume until the simulated trip times match TomTom's. It is what makes the “what if” answers worth trusting.
Green share
The part of a signal's green time given to the main road. 80% means the corridor gets 80% of the green and the side roads share the rest.
Reanalysis
Weather data rebuilt by a weather model from satellite and station records, on a grid of about 9 km. Good for “did it rain here that hour”, less exact than a rain gauge.
Mock mode
The API returns saved sample results instead of running the simulator (MOCK_SIM=1). Used while building screens and as a demo fallback. The status pill in the top bar says which mode is on.
